Country-Specific Privacy Guides

Understand the digital privacy landscape in your destination country.

🇨🇳 China

Risk Level: HIGH

Key Concerns: The Great Firewall blocks most Western websites and apps. Extensive surveillance infrastructure. VPNs are restricted.

Recommendations: Install multiple VPNs before arrival. Use offline maps. Avoid discussing sensitive topics. Consider a travel-only device.

🇷🇺 Russia

Risk Level: HIGH

Key Concerns: Increasing internet censorship. Data localization laws. Border officials may request device searches.

Recommendations: Use encrypted communications. Be cautious at borders. Keep social media activity clean. Have VPN ready.

🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates

Risk Level: HIGH

Key Concerns: VoIP calls blocked. Strict content laws. Surveillance common. VPN use restricted to licensed providers.

Recommendations: Use WhatsApp calls carefully. Avoid controversial content. Respect local laws strictly.

🇺🇸 United States

Risk Level: MEDIUM

Key Concerns: CBP has broad search powers at borders. Five Eyes intelligence sharing. No federal privacy law.

Recommendations: Know your rights at borders. Use encryption. Consider cloud backup before crossing. Be aware of data requests.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom

Risk Level: MEDIUM

Key Concerns: Investigatory Powers Act allows broad surveillance. Border searches possible. CCTV everywhere.

Recommendations: Use encryption. Be aware of anti-protest laws. Understand data retention requirements.

🇩🇪 Germany

Risk Level: LOW

Key Concerns: Strong privacy laws (GDPR). Some data retention requirements. Generally privacy-friendly.

Recommendations: GDPR protects you. Public Wi-Fi generally safe with VPN. Respect local recording laws.

🇯🇵 Japan

Risk Level: LOW

Key Concerns: Generally privacy-respectful. Some data retention. Low cybercrime rates.

Recommendations: Standard precautions apply. Public Wi-Fi widely available. Respect local etiquette.

🇮🇳 India

Risk Level: MEDIUM

Key Concerns: Increasing internet shutdowns. Aadhaar data concerns. Some content restrictions.

Recommendations: Use VPN for sensitive activities. Backup data. Be aware of local sensitivities.

🇧🇷 Brazil

Risk Level: MEDIUM

Key Concerns: High cybercrime rates. LGPD privacy law in effect. Occasional internet disruptions.

Recommendations: Be cautious with public Wi-Fi. Use VPN. Keep software updated. Monitor financial accounts.

🇦🇺 Australia

Risk Level: MEDIUM

Key Concerns: Five Eyes member. Mandatory data retention. Encryption access laws.

Recommendations: Use end-to-end encryption. Be aware of metadata collection. Understand border search powers.

🇫🇷 France

Risk Level: LOW

Key Concerns: EU GDPR protections. Tourist-targeted scams. Public Wi-Fi risks in cafes.

Recommendations: Keep devices secure in crowded areas. Use hotel safes. Avoid unsecured hotspots.

🇮🇹 Italy

Risk Level: LOW

Key Concerns: Pickpocketing in tourist areas. Unsecured café Wi-Fi.

Recommendations: Disable auto-connect. Stick to reputable establishments. Use VPN for banking.

🇪🇸 Spain

Risk Level: LOW

Key Concerns: Tourist phishing scams. Beach theft of devices.

Recommendations: Never leave devices unattended. Verify URLs before entering credentials.

🇹🇭 Thailand

Risk Level: MEDIUM

Key Concerns: Lèse-majesté laws. Content filtering. Social media monitoring.

Recommendations: Avoid political content online. Use VPN for unrestricted access. Respect local laws.

🇻🇳 Vietnam

Risk Level: HIGH

Key Concerns: Cybersecurity law requiring data localization. Social media monitoring. Blocked services.

Recommendations: Install VPN before arrival. Use encrypted messaging. Avoid criticizing government.

🇲🇽 Mexico

Risk Level: MEDIUM

Key Concerns: High cybercrime rates. Wi-Fi sniffing in tourist areas.

Recommendations: Avoid discussing travel plans publicly online. Use mobile data for sensitive tasks.

🇿🇦 South Africa

Risk Level: MEDIUM

Key Concerns: High cybercrime. Load shedding affecting security infrastructure.

Recommendations: Use UPS for routers. Avoid public internet terminals. Keep software updated.

🇨🇦 Canada

Risk Level: LOW

Key Concerns: Five Eyes alliance. Border device searches possible.

Recommendations: Know your border rights. Use strong passwords. Consider traveling with clean devices.

🇸🇬 Singapore

Risk Level: LOW

Key Concerns: Strict fake news laws. Surveillance cameras everywhere.

Recommendations: Be mindful of social media posts. Use encryption. Respect local regulations.

🇹🇷 Turkey

Risk Level: HIGH

Key Concerns: Social media blocks. Extensive surveillance. VoIP restrictions.

Recommendations: Use VPN for all social media. Encrypt all communications. Avoid sensitive topics.

🇪🇬 Egypt

Risk Level: HIGH

Key Concerns: VoIP blocking. Social media monitoring during protests.

Recommendations: Use encrypted messaging apps. Avoid political discussions online. Download offline maps.

🇲🇦 Morocco

Risk Level: MEDIUM

Key Concerns: Surveillance of dissent. Wi-Fi monitoring in public spaces.

Recommendations: Use encrypted communications. Avoid sensitive political topics online.

🇦🇷 Argentina

Risk Level: LOW

Key Concerns: Economic instability affecting infrastructure. Pickpocketing in Buenos Aires.

Recommendations: Use hotel safes. Avoid flashing expensive tech. Keep backups in cloud.

🇮🇩 Indonesia

Risk Level: MEDIUM

Key Concerns: Content filtering. Intermittent social media blocks.

Recommendations: Use VPN for consistent access. Respect local content laws. Download offline content.
