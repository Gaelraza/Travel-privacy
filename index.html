Country-Specific Privacy Guides

Understand the digital privacy landscape in your destination country.

🇨🇳 China

Risk Level: HIGH

Key Concerns: The Great Firewall blocks most Western websites and apps. Extensive surveillance infrastructure. VPNs are restricted.

Recommendations: Install multiple VPNs before arrival. Use offline maps. Avoid discussing sensitive topics. Consider a travel-only device.

🇷🇺 Russia

Risk Level: HIGH

Key Concerns: Increasing internet censorship. Data localization laws. Border officials may request device searches.

Recommendations: Use encrypted communications. Be cautious at borders. Keep social media activity clean. Have VPN ready.

🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates

Risk Level: HIGH

Key Concerns: VoIP calls blocked. Strict content laws. Surveillance common. VPN use restricted to licensed providers.

Recommendations: Use WhatsApp calls carefully. Avoid controversial content. Respect local laws strictly.

🇺🇸 United States

Risk Level: MEDIUM

Key Concerns: CBP has broad search powers at borders. Five Eyes intelligence sharing. No federal privacy law.

Recommendations: Know your rights at borders. Use encryption. Consider cloud backup before crossing. Be aware of data requests.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom

Risk Level: MEDIUM

Key Concerns: Investigatory Powers Act allows broad surveillance. Border searches possible. CCTV everywhere.

Recommendations: Use encryption. Be aware of anti-protest laws. Understand data retention requirements.

🇩🇪 Germany

Risk Level: LOW

Key Concerns: Strong privacy laws (GDPR). Some data retention requirements. Generally privacy-friendly.

Recommendations: GDPR protects you. Public Wi-Fi generally safe with VPN. Respect local recording laws.

🇯🇵 Japan

Risk Level: LOW

Key Concerns: Generally privacy-respectful. Some data retention. Low cybercrime rates.

Recommendations: Standard precautions apply. Public Wi-Fi widely available. Respect local etiquette.

🇮🇳 India

Risk Level: MEDIUM

Key Concerns: Increasing internet shutdowns. Aadhaar data concerns. Some content restrictions.

Recommendations: Use VPN for sensitive activities. Backup data. Be aware of local sensitivities.

🇧🇷 Brazil

Risk Level: MEDIUM

Key Concerns: High cybercrime rates. LGPD privacy law in effect. Occasional internet disruptions.

Recommendations: Be cautious with public Wi-Fi. Use VPN. Keep software updated. Monitor financial accounts.

🇦🇺 Australia

Risk Level: MEDIUM

Key Concerns: Five Eyes member. Mandatory data retention. Encryption access laws.

Recommendations: Use end-to-end encryption. Be aware of metadata collection. Understand border search powers.
